Security people always say it's not a matter of if, but when you get hacked. 

In September 2026, after almost seven years of finding vulnerabilities in other people's systems and warning them, we became the hackers that got hacked.

So what do hackers do when they get hacked? We handle it the way we think it should be handled, open, transparent and honest, even if it sucks. We posted updates while we were still figuring things out, shut down our infrastructure and assumed the worst until we knew more. Now we can tell the full story, from the Agentic Offensive Framework (ARTEX) the attacker used to which data got out and our lessons learned.

We wanted to learn from this type of attack, and oh, we did. We owe it to the people whose data got out, who never had a say in any of this, and to our volunteers, who never stopped working for a safer digital society, not even when the systems under attack were our own, to share everything we've seen and learned.

By being this open and transparent, we also hope to show the world that there is no need to be afraid or to feel ashamed. With attacks like this happening more and more often, it could happen to anyone, and it probably will. When it does, we encourage everyone to be brave and do the same.
